In his own words

"My father taught me this. His father taught him. Four generations. The masks used to fill this room."

— on his craft

"My son went to Colombo. Good job. I was proud. I thought my daughter would stay. She got married. I thought — someone will come."

— on the day he realised

"Nobody came. But I kept the chair. I always kept the chair. I don't know why. Maybe I still believe someone will sit down."

— on the empty chair
